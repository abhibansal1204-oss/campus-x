⚡ Smart Priority Engine

CAMPUS-X problem priority calculate karne ke liye:


Priority Score = Urgency × 4 + Students Affected × 2 + Deadline Importance × 3


  • Score 25 or above → High Priority
  • Score 15–24 → Medium Priority
  • Score below 15 → Low Priority
